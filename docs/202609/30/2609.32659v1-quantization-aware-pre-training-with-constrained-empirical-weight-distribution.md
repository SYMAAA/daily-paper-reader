## 摘要
量化感知预训练（QAPT）可以提高 DNN 的推理效率，但一种称为舍入边界权重振荡的问题行为会向训练过程引入有害噪声，并显著降低收敛速度。现有方法虽然可以减少这种有害噪声，但要么引入额外超参数或内存开销，要么无法持续提升模型精度。在这项工作中，我们提出了约束经验权重分布优化（CEWT），这是首个无需超参数、无内存开销且能持续提升 QAPT 性能的振荡抑制方法：一种优化器更新后步骤，将权重投影到权重空间中经验分布（直方图）与零均值高斯分布匹配的最近点。我们的关键洞见是，许多量化器在设计时隐含假设待量化数据是来自零均值高斯的样本的排列，而在 QAPT 期间这一假设并不成立。通过将零均值高斯先验作为硬约束强制执行，CEWT 能够抑制这种有害噪声。在多种 SOTA 量化器与超球面优化器组合上的实证结果表明，以训练时间几何平均增加 4% 为代价，CEWT 能够持续降低低精度（激活和权重低至 1 比特，参数量最高 610M）LLaMA/GPT 模型的预训练困惑度（平均降低 2.5，最高降低 21 个点），且不引入任何超参数或存储开销。代码见 https://github.com/1733116199/cewt

## Abstract
Quantization-Aware Pre-Training (QAPT) can increase the inference efficiency of DNNs, but a problematic behaviour known as rounding boundary weight oscillation can introduce detrimental noise into the training process and significantly reduce convergence speed. While existing methods can reduce this detrimental noise, they either introduce additional hyperparameters or memory overhead, or cannot consistently improve model accuracy. In this work, we propose optimization with $\textbf{C}$onstrained $\textbf{E}$mpirical $\textbf{W}$eight dis$\textbf{T}$ribution (CEWT), the first hyperparameter-free memory-overhead-free oscillation suppression method that consistently improves QAPT performance: an optimizer post-update step that projects weights to the nearest point in weight space whose empirical distribution (histogram) matches a zero-mean Gaussian. Our key insight is many quantizers are designed with the implicit assumption that the to-be-quantized data are permutations of samples from a zero-mean Gaussian, and this assumption is not true during QAPT. By enforcing the zero-mean Gaussian prior as a hard constraint, CEWT can suppress this detrimental noise. Empirical results on various combinations of SOTA quantizers and hypersphere optimizers suggest, that with a geomean increase of 4% in training time, CEWT can consistently reduce the pre-training perplexity (by an average of 2.5 and up to 21 points) of low-precision (down to 1-bit activations and weights and up to 610M parameters) LLaMA/GPT models without introducing any hyperparameters or storage overhead. Code is available at https://github.com/1733116199/cewt

## 摘要
近年来，自回归视觉生成（ARVG）模型已成为高质量图像合成中具有竞争力的范式。然而，其巨大的计算和内存需求使其难以部署在资源受限的边缘设备上。训练后量化（PTQ）为降低 ARVG 模型的计算和内存成本提供了一种有前景的解决方案，但现有方法在低比特量化下往往会出现显著的图像质量下降。本文提出了一种面向 ARVG 的免训练 PTQ 框架，由尾部感知的有界初始化网格细化（T-BGR）和由粗到细的激活精度调度（CAPS）组成。T-BGR 为权重量化器校准引入尾部感知的重建目标，对分布尾部系数赋予更高重要性，并在不引入额外参数或高精度分支的情况下保留细粒度信息。CAPS 通过联合考虑不同生成阶段的重要性和计算成本，执行由粗到细的激活精度调度，从而在给定比特预算下实现高效的混合精度分配。在多个 ARVG 主干网络上的大量实验表明，我们的框架在激进低比特量化下持续提升鲁棒性。值得注意的是，在 Infinity 上，我们的框架在有效 W4A5.6 激活精度下实现了接近全精度的图像生成质量，而现有方法在 W4A6 下仍表现出明显的质量下降或不一致性能。代码可在 https://github.com/lxm-lc/HGA-Quant 获取。

## Abstract
Recently, AutoRegressive Visual Generation (ARVG) models have emerged as a competitive paradigm for high-quality image synthesis. However, their substantial computational and memory demands make them difficult to deploy on resource-constrained edge devices. Post-Training Quantization (PTQ) provides a promising solution to reduce the computational and memory costs of ARVG models, yet existing methods often suffer from substantial image quality degradation under low-bit quantization. In this paper, we propose a training-free PTQ framework for ARVG, consisting of Tail-Aware Bounded-init Grid Refinement (T-BGR) and Coarse-to-Fine Activation Precision Scheduling (CAPS). T-BGR introduces a tail-aware reconstruction objective for weight quantizer calibration, assigning greater importance to distribution-tail coefficients and preserving fine-grained information without introducing additional parameters or high-precision branches. CAPS performs coarse-to-fine activation precision scheduling by jointly considering the importance and computational cost of different generation stages, enabling efficient mixed-precision allocation under a given bit budget. Extensive experiments on multiple ARVG backbones demonstrate that our framework consistently improves robustness under aggressive low-bit quantization. Notably, on Infinity, our framework achieves near full-precision image generation quality at an effective W4A5.6 activation precision, while existing methods at W4A6 still exhibit noticeable quality degradation or inconsistent performance. Code is available at https://github.com/lxm-lc/HGA-Quant.

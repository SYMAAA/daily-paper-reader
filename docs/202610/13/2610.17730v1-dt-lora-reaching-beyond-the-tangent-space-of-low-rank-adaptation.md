## 摘要
低秩适配（LoRA）往往不如全量微调。我们从切空间视角研究这一性能差距：因子化更新被限制在当前低秩因子的切空间内，导致该空间之外的梯度分量未被利用。对十种代表性 LoRA 变体的分析表明，这些残差分量占据了大部分梯度能量，并表现出很强的谱集中性。受此发现启发，我们提出动态切空间 LoRA（Dynamic Tangent LoRA, DT-LoRA），它将固定的秩预算划分为稳定分支和动态分支，以捕获主要的残差梯度方向。我们证明，这些方向定义了在正交补与秩约束下局部二次模型的最优更新。无矩阵随机化 SVD 可在不显式构造残差梯度的情况下恢复这些方向，而受控回写则保留累积更新并在分配秩内稳定训练。DT-LoRA 相比 LoRA 不增加可训练参数或推理成本。在计算机视觉和自然语言处理任务上的实验表明，其相较 LoRA 及其变体取得一致提升。此外，尽管 DT-LoRA 是一种通用适配方法，其在语义分割的开放词汇域泛化任务上优于任务特定方法，取得了最先进的结果。代码见 https://github.com/Snipine/DT-LoRA。

## Abstract
Low-rank adaptation (LoRA) often underperforms full fine-tuning. We investigate this performance gap through a tangent-space lens: factorized updates are confined to the tangent space of the current low-rank factors, leaving gradient components outside this space unexploited. An analysis of ten representative LoRA variants reveals that these residual components account for most of the gradient energy and exhibit strong spectral concentration. Motivated by this finding, we propose Dynamic Tangent LoRA (DT-LoRA), which partitions a fixed rank budget into a stable branch and a dynamic branch to capture the principal residual gradient directions. We show that these directions define the optimal update of a local quadratic model under orthogonal-complement and rank constraints. Matrix-free randomized SVD recovers them without materializing the residual gradient, while controlled write-back preserves accumulated updates and stabilizes training within the allocated rank. DT-LoRA adds no trainable parameters or inference cost over LoRA. Experiments across computer vision and natural language processing tasks demonstrate consistent improvements over LoRA and its variants. Moreover, despite being a general-purpose adaptation method, DT-LoRA outperforms task-specific approaches to open-vocabulary domain generalization for semantic segmentation, achieving state-of-the-art results. Code is available at https://github.com/Snipine/DT-LoRA.

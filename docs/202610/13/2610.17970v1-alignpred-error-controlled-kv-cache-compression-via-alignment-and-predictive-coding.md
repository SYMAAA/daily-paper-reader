## 摘要
长时间运行的智能体在多个轮次和会话之间会保留 KV 缓存数小时甚至数天。压缩这些持久化缓存需要在降低存储占用的同时控制重构误差。我们提出 AlignPred，一种在可配置的 KV 重构误差预算下将局部对齐与预测编码相结合的编解码器。局部正交变换将相关的注意力头或层对齐到同一坐标系中，使 AlignPred 能够将逐 token 预测与跨注意力头或层的同 token 预测相结合。残差在有误差界的格点上量化并进行熵编码，无需学习型预测器、编码端模式搜索或大规模联合变换。在三种注意力机制下，在相近的实测重构误差下，AlignPred 所需的存储空间少于所评估的基线方法。与所评估的 CPU 编解码器实现相比，其解码速度至少快 1.6 倍，同时编码时间相当或更优。在 128K 上下文的 LongBench-Pro 上，AlignPred 在显著压缩的工作点下仍能达到接近原生水平的任务分数。AlignPred 与 SGLang HiCache 的集成相比原生 HiCache 将持久化目标 KV 存储降低 42–72%，且延迟相当。

## Abstract
Long-running agents retain KV caches across turns and sessions for hours or days. Compressing these persistent caches requires reducing storage while controlling reconstruction error. We present AlignPred, a codec that combines local alignment with predictive coding under a configurable KV reconstruction-error budget. Local orthogonal transforms align related heads or layers into a common coordinate frame, allowing AlignPred to combine token-wise prediction with same-token prediction across heads or layers. Residuals are quantized on an error-bounded lattice and entropy-coded, without learned predictors, encoder-side mode search, or large joint transforms. Across three attention mechanisms, AlignPred requires less storage than the evaluated baselines at comparable measured reconstruction error. Against the evaluated CPU codec implementations, it decodes at least 1.6 times faster while matching or improving encode time. AlignPred achieves near-native task scores at substantially compressed operating points on LongBench-Pro at 128K context. AlignPred's SGLang HiCache integration reduces persistent target-KV storage by 42-72% relative to native HiCache, with comparable latency.

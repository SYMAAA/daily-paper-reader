## 摘要
直通估计器（Straight-Through Estimator, STE）是量化感知训练（QAT）中广泛使用的启发式方法，但其代理梯度可能与底层量化目标存在显著不匹配，导致更新噪声大和参数振荡，尤其是在超低位宽情形下。我们提出量化感知最小范数优化器（Quantization-Aware Minimal-Norm Optimizer, Q-MINO），一种时间束方法，结合梯度共识、状态漂移正则化和对齐约束，从近期优化状态中构造稳定化的最小范数更新方向。Q-MINO 使用带热启动的 Frank--Wolfe 过程以及可行回退初始化来求解所得的约束子问题。理论上，我们通过随机 Lyapunov Kurdyka--Łojasiewicz (KL) 框架证明 Q-MINO 达到渐近邻域收敛。此外，我们详细介绍了 Q-MINO 在不同量化下的数值实验。

## Abstract
The Straight-Through Estimator (STE) is a widely used heuristic for Quantization-Aware Training (QAT), but its surrogate gradients can exhibit substantial mismatch with the underlying quantized objective, leading to noisy updates and parameter oscillations, particularly in ultra-low-bit regimes. We propose the Quantization-Aware Minimal-Norm Optimizer (Q-MINO), a temporal bundle method that combines gradient consensus, state-drift regularization, and an alignment constraint to construct stabilized, minimum-norm update directions from recent optimization states. Q-MINO solves the resulting constrained subproblem using a warm-started Frank--Wolfe procedure with a feasible fallback initialization. Theoretically, via a stochastic Lyapunov Kurdyka--Łojasiewicz (KL) framework, we show that Q-MINO achieves asymptotic neighborhood convergence. Moreover, we detail numerical experiments with Q-MINO at various quantizations.

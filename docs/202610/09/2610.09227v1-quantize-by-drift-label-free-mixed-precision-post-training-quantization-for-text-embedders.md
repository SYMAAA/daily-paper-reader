## 摘要
混合精度训练后量化需要逐模块的敏感度信号；对于文本嵌入器而言，最直观的信号——量化某个模块所付出的检索质量代价——需要相关性标签，而部署中很少具备这些标签。我们测量了一种无标签替代信号：量化诱导的表示漂移，其做法是量化单个模块、重新编码语料，并记录输出嵌入相对于全精度位置移动了多远。其特殊之处在于所观测的对象：稠密检索器据以排序的部署输出表示。在五个开发用嵌入器上，配置级漂移将采样的混合精度方案按留出检索质量排序，宏观 Spearman 相关系数为 0.911；该敏感度在可用范围内可跨校准语料和检索领域迁移；模块漂移在秩上一致地组合，但在数值上并非如此；由相关性导出的敏感度没有带来一致的增益。该方法是在硬性打包字节预算下的一种加性分配，无需标签，也无需搜索。在三个嵌入器上——直到方法、基线和假设被冻结并封存之前都保持未触碰——针对先前 LieQ 准则的预注册方向性假设成立（在主预算下 3/3，无崩溃），且漂移分数在 2/3 的情况下高于双侧 LieQ steelman；但在主预算下，漂移方法在三个嵌入器上的数值均低于同预算的均匀精度（分别低 0.99、0.85、1.01 分），尽管它按设计降低了模块漂移和整模型漂移。因此，输出漂移是一种稳健的粗粒度敏感度信号，而非普遍最优的分配目标：它避免了迁移的有符号几何自适应所带来的灾难性失败，并且在均匀方案崩溃的紧张预算下仍可使用，但在强均匀工作点附近的细粒度重分配仍悬而未决。

## Abstract
Mixed-precision post-training quantization needs a per-module sensitivity signal; for a text embedder the obvious one -- the retrieval quality a module costs when quantized -- needs relevance labels that deployments rarely have. We measure a label-free substitute: quantization-induced representation drift, obtained by quantizing one module, re-encoding the corpus, and recording how far the output embeddings moved from their full-precision positions. What is specific is the observable: the deployed output representation a dense retriever ranks with. Across five development embedders, configuration-level drift orders sampled mixed-precision plans against held-out retrieval quality at a macro Spearman of 0.911, the sensitivity transports across calibration corpora and retrieval domains in the usable regime, module drifts compose rank-consistently but not numerically, and relevance-derived sensitivity adds no consistent value. The method is one additive allocation under a hard packed-byte budget, with no labels and no search. On three embedders held untouched until method, baselines and hypotheses were frozen and sealed, the pre-registered directional hypothesis against the prior LieQ criterion holds (3/3 at the main budget, no collapse) and drift scores above a two-sided LieQ steelman in 2/3; but at the main budget drift is numerically lower than same-budget uniform precision on all three (-0.99, -0.85, -1.01 points), having reduced module and whole-model drift as designed. Output drift is thus a robust coarse sensitivity signal, not a universally optimal allocation objective: it avoids the catastrophic failures of the transferred signed-geometry adaptation and can remain usable at stressed budgets where uniform collapses, but fine-grained redistribution around a strong uniform operating point remains unresolved.
